# Privacy and public-repository boundary

This skill may be published publicly, but operational data must remain local.

Never commit or publish:

- employee names, IDs, departments, or personal learning details;
- OA process numbers, applicants, titles, or row-level execution data;
- expenses, income, budget paths, or transaction details;
- real organization exclusion and mapping tables;
- filled Excel/PPT files or internal branded templates unless the owner has explicitly approved publication;
- intermediate inspection artifacts that contain real values.

Public files may contain:

- generic processing rules and formulas;
- file-discovery and validation scripts;
- fictional examples;
- fully sanitized blank templates whose publication is explicitly approved.

Before publishing, search the repository for names, IDs, currency amounts, absolute user paths, organization labels, and copied workbook/PPT metadata. If uncertain, omit the asset and require a local template at runtime.
